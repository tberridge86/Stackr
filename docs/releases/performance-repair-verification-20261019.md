## Phone measurement protocol

Use the same declared journey and cache/network condition for each block. Record build, device model, operating system, network, cache condition, failures, retries and cancellations. Network/cache labels supplied by a tester are declarations, not detected or verified conditions; changing a label does not clear a cache. Include the actual input/tap and debounce. Inspector-request timing begins after the recognition of its opening gesture; separately measure the complete physical gesture.

Collect at least100 observations per journey and cache condition before calculating p95. Preserve failed/cancelled trials and report their denominator independently of successful latency. Export bounded local observations before retention loss and retain each block's identity; do not combine overlapping exports as independent trials. Disclose dropped observations rather than treating missing trials as successes. Automated fixtures are not phone samples.

Acceptance remains: general warm p95 under1000ms and cold p95 under2000ms; applicable binder memory complete-render p95 at most100ms, persisted at most200ms p95, and each applicable repeated warm complete-network sample at most500ms. JS readiness/viewability and native rendering must be reported separately. No100-trial native cohort or passing phone p95 is claimed here.

Repeat opening, dismissal, background/resume, offline retry, pagination and account switching. Check native resource/sensor cleanup and physical feedback independently. Source fixes cannot close showcase or haptic acceptance without phone evidence from the separately approved candidate.
