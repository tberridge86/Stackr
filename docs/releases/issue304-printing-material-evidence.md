## Representative finish policy

The same rule applies to cosmos, reverse, diagonal/line, textured and radiant finishes: canonical finish metadata can identify the class, but a printing-specific visual effect requires an exact reviewed material pack bound to the canonical card/language/variant/finish and the artwork URI that actually rendered. Unknown finishes and known finishes without a reviewed pack remain visually plain apart from physical card tilt/depth.
