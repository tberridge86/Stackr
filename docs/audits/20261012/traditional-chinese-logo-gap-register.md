## What this register means

The successful snapshot contained 83 `zh-tw` factual set rows and 47 approved, publicly deliverable `set_logo` rows. The 36 records below were absent from that public logo manifest at that time. **Absent public metadata does not mean the mark does not exist elsewhere, was never sourced, or lacks rights.**

For each of the 36, the exact `(language=zh-tw, set ID)` did not match a tracked source-art entry in the reviewed Traditional Chinese local-pack manifest, which contains only SCA, SCB, SCC, SCD, S8a and SN. Those six exact bytes have source-review evidence and are already publicly represented in the snapshot; none is one of the 36 gaps. The Simplified Chinese pack was deliberately excluded: its identities and review scope are `zh-cn`, so code/name similarity cannot establish a Traditional Chinese match.

This is therefore a **publication/source-evidence register**, not an acquisition finding. Primary source, licence, rights, and any owner-supplied-but-untracked material are unknown unless explicitly recorded below.
