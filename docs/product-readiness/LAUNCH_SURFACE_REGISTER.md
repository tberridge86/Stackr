# Stackr beta launch-surface register

**Decision date:** 27 August 2026
**Scope:** the upcoming beta; this is the single source of truth for what users may see.
**Release rule:** an `Expose` decision still requires green CI, staging smoke coverage, authorised data access, and no P0/P1 defect. A failed condition changes the decision to `Hide`; it does not create a partially working teaser.
**Current Gate 0 status:** **GO — 100%.** PRs #59 and #60 completed the hardening and staging-verifier closeout. Commerce remains intentionally locked; Gate 0 completion proves the lock, not full-product release readiness.

## Decision key

| Decision | Launch behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Expose** | Visible to the intended beta audience and included in navigation, onboarding, support and launch content. |
| **Conditional** | Default off. Visible only to the named cohort when the enabling evidence is recorded; the route and writes stay unavailable to everyone else. |
| **Hide** | No navigation or promotional affordance. Deep links fail safely, provider calls cannot execute, and related financial/order state cannot be written. |

Financial commitment is deliberately last: Stripe checkout, Stripe Connect onboarding/payouts, Shippo live rates, labels and tracking remain **Hide** until every non-financial beta surface has passed closeout. Gate 0 must be closed without provider accounts, plans or credentials. Provider-account setup, paid-plan commitment and live credential entry are the founder's final action, not a prerequisite for the two-week quality push.

## Financial activation sequence (last closeout item)

1. Mark every non-financial `Expose` row passed and record evidence for each enabled `Conditional` row.
2. Close Gate 0 on the exact merged build: re-run staging hard-lock tests and confirm zero Stripe/Shippo execution and no forbidden commerce-state mutation while locked.
3. Finish the provider-independent order state machine, amount authority, returns/refunds, disputes, reconciliation, support and rollback rehearsals; prepare test-mode cohort flags with all financial surfaces still `Hide`.
4. Founder accepts pricing/fees and, as the founder's final action, completes Stripe and Shippo account or plan setup and enters credentials directly into the approved secret stores.
5. Engineering activates one capability at a time behind a cohort flag: seller Connect/KYC and payout readiness first; checkout/payment into an authoritative order second; paid-order-bound rates, labels and tracking third; payout only after the defined fulfilment/settlement event. A failed smoke test returns the surface to `Hide`.

No launch copy may say that checkout, payouts, live shipping, labels or orders are “coming soon.” Until activated, those actions are absent.
