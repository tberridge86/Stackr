Product guide

Product information

Check the language, pack count and included items for each product.

What’s the difference between an ETB and a bundle?

The booster bundles here contain six packs from one set. The standard Elite Trainer Boxes shown contain nine packs, a promo card and accessories such as sleeves and dice. An ETB’s outer box can be used for storage.

Will the packs contain the card I’m looking for?

Booster contents are random. A Pokémon pictured on the pack or box does not guarantee that card is inside. A separately included promo card is identified in the product description.

Are these Pokémon Center editions?

The Elite Trainer Boxes shown are standard English-language retail editions. Pokémon Center exclusives are not included in this preview. Each basket item is one retail unit; any assorted artwork is explained in its description.

What about delivery and returns?

Delivery charges are not set. Services, dispatch times and final returns information will be published before orders open. Contact Hello@StackrTCG.com with a question.

Can I place an order yet?

Not yet. You can browse all __PRODUCT_COUNT__ products and try the bag and saved items, but checkout does not take payments or submit an order. Current exact retail references determine the prices shown. Availability is managed by the shop owner.
