THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE BINDERS

The story of StackR

An old collection, a new collector and a hobby to share.

Stackr started with me and my son, and a hobby I thought I’d left behind. As he discovered Pokémon, I dusted off the incomplete Neo collection I’d bought from QVC years earlier. Within just a few weeks, his knowledge of the Pokémon, the sets and the cards had me struggling to keep up. I’d opened an old collection; he’d got me collecting again.

It became something that was ours to get excited about together. Comparing cards, learning from each other and sharing the anticipation of another pack. That time together is where Stackr began.

For every kind of collector

But Stackr is for all of us. For the master-setters, the chasers and the collectors who know when to walk away from a deal. For the budget rippers and the grail hunters. For the yellow-border loyalists, the rainbow collectors and the tournament competitors. For the original 151 collectors, and those whose adventures began with Silver and Gold.

From Evolving Cries to Chilling Pain, for everyone who’s said “never again” and then opened another pack. For the vending-machine hopefuls and the one-card-away completionist. For those counting Gold Stars and those dreaming of a shadowless Charizard. For the pristine binders, the battered childhood collections and the favourite card you wouldn’t sell for anything.

We work with reputable wholesalers to source English and international Pokémon TCG products. Our aim is to offer 10% below a checked UK retail reference, reviewed weekly. Just as importantly, we want to keep products in stock for people who love collecting. Registering your interest helps us understand what you actually want to find here.

Whether you’re here for the value, the artwork, the battles or simply the time spent together, you’re welcome at Stackr.

The best part of this hobby isn’t always what you pull. It’s who you turn to when you pull it.
